## candle: ModernBERT sparse encoder

`crates/inl-sparse/src/modernbert.rs` implements the ModernBERT masked-LM with
`ShiftedTopK` SPLADE pooling that the default sparse checkpoint family ships.
It is written against that checkpoint family rather than reusing
`candle_transformers::models::modernbert`, which applies no padding mask to the
sliding window of padded keys and has no MLM-logit chunking. Weight names and
numerics follow the Hugging Face reference; the golden test reproduces its
output.

- Library: [candle](https://github.com/huggingface/candle) (`candle-core`,
  `candle-nn`)
- License: Apache License, Version 2.0 or MIT

## google/embeddinggemma-2: EmbeddingGemma 2

`crates/inl-text/src/gemma2.rs` (text tower) and `crates/inl-vision/` (vision
tower, image and video preprocessing, multimodal merge) are written against the
Hugging Face `transformers` reference (`EmbeddingGemma2Model`, and the Gemma 4
vision modules it composes) and the checkpoint's Sentence-Transformers
pipeline. The golden tests reproduce their output, stage by stage.

- Reference: `transformers` (<https://github.com/huggingface/transformers>),
  Apache License, Version 2.0
- Model: `google/embeddinggemma-2`, Apache License, Version 2.0

## Test fixtures

`crates/inl-text/tests/fixtures/tiny_embeddinggemma2` and
`crates/inl-sparse/tests/fixtures/tiny_splade` are tiny randomly initialised
checkpoints generated by `scripts/gen_tiny_*_fixture.py`; they contain no
upstream weights. `golden_*.json` files hold outputs of the reference
implementations on synthetic inputs.
